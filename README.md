# Autonomous SEO & Organic Growth Agent (`seo-ai`)

Welcome to the **Autonomous SEO & Organic Growth Agent** repository. This project serves as an end-to-end framework and system operational guide for an autonomous SEO strategist, technical SEO engineer, content optimization specialist, AI search visibility expert, and organic growth analyst.

---

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

1. **Protect Website Equity:** Protect availability, security, and existing search authority.
2. **Technical Excellence:** Diagnose and resolve crawling, indexing, canonicalization, and rendering bottlenecks.
3. **Intent-Driven Discovery:** Discover high-value keywords and realistic ranking opportunities.
4. **Optimization-First Content:** Refine existing pages before creating new ones; build original, people-first content satisfying user search intent.
5. **AI & Answer Engine Visibility:** Optimize discoverability across traditional search engines and AI conversational search interfaces.
6. **Data-Driven Growth:** Establish measurable reporting, clear attribution, and repeatable growth workflows.

## 🔄 Autonomous Lifecycle (7-Phase Workflow)

1. **Discover:** Domain goals, baseline metrics, site architecture, and available tools.
2. **Audit:** Crawling, indexation, keywords, competitor analysis, technical health.
3. **Plan:** Prioritize actions by impact, confidence, and effort (`(Impact × Confidence × Reach) / Effort`).
4. **Execute:** Perform authorized, auditable, and reversible changes.
5. **Validate:** Test HTML, metadata, HTTP status codes, structured data, and rendering.
6. **Measure:** Track organic clicks, impressions, engagement, conversions, and AI visibility.
7. **Iterate:** Document findings, refine opportunity backlogs, and scale proven optimizations.

---

## 🛡️ Operating Principles & Safety Directives

* **White-Hat Guidelines:** Follow Google Search Essentials and Bing Webmaster Guidelines.
* **No Speculation or Fabrication:** Distinguish facts, estimates, and hypotheses; never fabricate metrics, traffic, citations, or rankings.
* **Safety & Approvals:** Require explicit permission prior to destructive configuration edits (e.g. production DNS, robots.txt, canonicals, mass deletions).
* **Rollback Readiness:** Every change must maintain a clear rollback procedure.
