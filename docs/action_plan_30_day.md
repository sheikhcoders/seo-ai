# 30-Day SEO Action Plan

## Executive Summary
This 30-Day Action Plan establishes the foundational SEO operational framework for `seo-ai`. The plan focuses on setting up automated technical audits, baseline keyword tracking, content quality guidelines, and analytics monitoring. Execution will prioritize protecting existing site equity while targeting quick-win technical fixes and high-intent keyword opportunities.

---

## Evidence
- **Environment Baseline:** New repository initialization (`seo-ai`).
- **Data Integrations:** Pending Search Console, Google Analytics, and Crawl API access.
- **Initial Focus:** Operational readiness, protocol documentation, template standardization, and risk mitigation.

---

## Priority Actions

### Week 1: Foundation & Technical Baseline (Days 1–7)
- **Action 1.1: Environment & Tool Verification**
  - Priority: Critical | Impact: High | Confidence: High | Effort: Low
  - Role: Technical SEO Engineer
  - Validation Criteria: Confirm accessible APIs, credentials, and repository permissions.
- **Action 1.2: Technical Baseline Crawl**
  - Priority: Critical | Impact: High | Confidence: High | Effort: Medium
  - Role: Technical SEO Engineer
  - Validation Criteria: Execute crawl check on core pages; verify HTTP response codes, canonical tags, and robots.txt compliance.

### Week 2: Keyword Intent & Content Inventory (Days 8–15)
- **Action 2.1: Seed Keyword & Intent Mapping**
  - Priority: High | Impact: High | Confidence: High | Effort: Medium
  - Role: Keyword Research Specialist
  - Validation Criteria: Complete keyword matrix classifying informational vs transactional intent and mapping primary queries to target URLs.
- **Action 2.2: Existing Content Audit**
  - Priority: High | Impact: Medium | Confidence: High | Effort: Medium
  - Role: Content Specialist
  - Validation Criteria: Evaluate current published pages for E-E-A-T, thin content, duplicate tags, and missing metadata.

### Week 3: On-Page Optimization & Schema Implementation (Days 16–22)
- **Action 3.1: Metadata & Headings Refresh**
  - Priority: High | Impact: High | Confidence: High | Effort: Low
  - Role: Content Optimization Specialist
  - Validation Criteria: Optimize title tags, meta descriptions, and H1 tags on high-priority landing pages.
- **Action 3.2: Structured Data Markup**
  - Priority: Medium | Impact: Medium | Confidence: High | Effort: Low
  - Role: Technical SEO Engineer
  - Validation Criteria: Implement and validate JSON-LD schema (Organization, WebSite, Article/Product).

### Week 4: Analytics Integration & Opportunity Backlog (Days 23–30)
- **Action 4.1: Reporting Dashboard Setup**
  - Priority: High | Impact: High | Confidence: High | Effort: Medium
  - Role: Organic Growth Analyst
  - Validation Criteria: Baseline metrics recorded for clicks, impressions, CTR, and conversions.
- **Action 4.2: Growth Engine Backlog Prioritization**
  - Priority: Medium | Impact: High | Confidence: High | Effort: Low
  - Role: SEO Director
  - Validation Criteria: Rank expansion opportunities using `(Impact × Confidence × Reach) / Effort` scoring formula.
